# 🧠 Idea Backlog (Prioritized)

This document is a catch-all for mechanics, systems, and thematic ideas. Items are grouped by priority to guide implementation without losing long-term vision.

---

# 🚀 P0 — MVP (Build Now / Next)

These directly impact whether the game becomes **fun and replayable**.

---

## 1. Event System Expansion (HIGH PRIORITY)

Events should introduce:

- Negative outcomes (not all rewards)
- Risk/reward decisions
- Tradeoffs that affect the run

Examples:

- Gain a powerful card → add a curse
- Heal → lose max HP
- Upgrade card → take damage

👉 This is one of the fastest ways to make the game feel like a _real roguelike_

---

## 2. Ocean / Sea Condition System (LIGHT VERSION)

Introduce a **simple global modifier per battle**:

Start small (1–2 conditions max):

- Stormy → occasional bonus damage (both sides)
- Calm → +1 energy per turn

👉 Don’t overbuild—just prove the system is interesting

---

## 3. Enemy Variety & Pressure

Expand enemies to:

- Apply debuffs
- Disrupt player strategy (especially cannon flow)

Add:

- 1–2 new enemy types
- At least 1 enemy that directly interferes with your core mechanic

👉 If enemies don’t pressure the player, nothing else matters

---

## 4. Ship Identity (Cannon Ship Polish)

Refine the current ship:

- Make charge system feel impactful
- Ensure:
  - Setup turns feel risky
  - Payoff turns feel strong

👉 This is your “fun test”—everything builds on this

---

# ⚙️ P1 — Next Layer (After MVP Feels Good)

These deepen the game and add meaningful variation.

---

## 5. Crew System (Synergy + Tradeoffs)

Crew members:

- Provide passive bonuses
- Introduce tradeoffs

Examples:

- +Damage, -Energy
- +Energy, no healing at ports
- Stronger status effects, weaker base attacks

👉 This becomes your **build identity layer**

--- s

## 6. Ship Maneuvering & Boarding (MAYBE)

Add limited mechanics for:

- Maneuvering (defensive/positional effects)
- Boarding (crew-based actions)

Potential effects:

- Steal resources
- Apply debuffs
- Interrupt enemy turns

👉 Keep this **light** initially—don’t build a full subsystem yet

---

## 7. Expanded Enemy Types (Fantasy Layer)

Introduce more variety:

- Sea monsters (Kraken, etc.)
- Undead ships
- Magic-based enemies

👉 Focus on **mechanics first**, visuals/lore second

---

## 8. Persistent Run Modifiers

Events or choices that affect future encounters:

Examples:

- Stronger enemies for next 3 battles
- Increased rewards with added risk
- Ongoing debuffs

👉 Adds long-term strategy to runs

---

# 🧪 P2 — Experimental / High Risk Ideas

These are interesting but could easily hurt the game if done poorly.

---

## 9. Ghost Run Mechanic

After losing:

- Replay run
- Fight a ghost version of your previous ship

Potential:

- Uses your old deck
- Scales with progress

👉 Very cool, but not needed early

---

## 10. Crew Resource / Supply System

Crew requires:

- Supplies (food, morale, etc.)

⚠️ Risk:

- Can feel like tedious micromanagement

👉 Only pursue if it clearly adds meaningful decisions

---

## 11. Spell System (Secondary Combat Layer)

Introduce spells as:

- Buff / utility focused cards
- Less direct damage, more control

👉 This likely overlaps with future ship designs—don’t force it early

---

# 💤 P3 — Later / Polish

---

## 12. Tutorial / Onboarding

Simple introduction to:

- Combat
- Cards
- Core mechanics

👉 Important, but only after the game is actually fun

---

# 🧭 Final Direction (Important)

### Your current focus should be:

1. Cannon ship feels good
2. Events introduce tension
3. Enemies force decisions

---

### What to actively avoid right now:

- Building multiple ships
- Adding too many systems at once
- Over-designing mechanics you haven’t played yet

---

# 💬 Honest Take

If you execute just **P0 well**, you’ll have:
👉 A genuinely playable, replayable prototype

If you try to mix P0 + P1 + P2:
👉 You’ll stall in complexity before it becomes fun
