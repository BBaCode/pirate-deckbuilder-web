## Card Types (baseline + light twists)

### Attack

- Direct damage to enemies
- May apply status effects

Twist: Some attacks scale based on conditions
e.g. bonus damage if enemy is poisoned, weakened, more cannons etc.

### Skill

- Defense, utility, draw, manipulation
- No direct damage

Twist: Skills are your control layer

- Deck filtering (draw/discard)
- Status manipulation (cleanse, amplify)

### Power

- Persistent effect for the combat
- Played once per fight

Twist: Powers define your build direction
e.g. “Whenever you apply Poison, gain 1 block”

### Status (combat-only negative cards)

- Clutter hand/deck
- Temporary

Examples:

- Burn → damages you when drawn
- Jammed Cannon → unplayable for 1 turn
- Panic → forces discard

### Curse (persistent negative cards)

- Stay in deck until removed
- Acquired mainly via events
- Stronger than status cards
