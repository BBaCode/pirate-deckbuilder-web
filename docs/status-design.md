## Status Effects (Core Combat Layer)

### Priority:

- Poison: damage over time. Could actually be burning?
- Vulnerable: take more damage. Could be ship related like "spreading gun powder" or something to the enemy which causes more damage per attack. Could also be a magical thing like a curse on the ship
- Weak: reduced outgoing damage. I think this would probably be disabling a cannon(s) temporarily

### Needed at some point

- Flooding: Could be similar to poison or could be more focused on reducing energy because crew is "patching" or whatever. the kind of waters your in could change what the effect is. (boiling water causes damage, freezing water causes energy loss or weak)

### Something to consider but more complex

- Paralysis: Chance to lose turn, magic based?
- Morale: Based on spoils or some other meter, maybe synergy or past decisions
