## Enemy Types

### Basic Enemies

- Simple patterns
- Teach mechanics
- Low punishment

### Elite Enemies

- Stronger stats
- Introduce mechanic pressure
- heavy debuffs
- scaling damage
- deck disruption

### Boss Enemies

- Define the run
- Multi-phase or scaling mechanics

## Enemy Intent System (CRITICAL)

Each turn, enemy shows:

Attack
Defend
Apply debuff
Buff self

This creates:

Decision-making
Counterplay

## Enemy Archetypes (start with 3)

Bruiser → high damage
Controller → debuffs/status spam
Scaler → gets stronger over time (builds more cannons every turn, or uses increasingly damaging cannonballs)
